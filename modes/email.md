# Email

Email is more durable and usually warmer than Slack. Make the message easy to
scan and act on.

- Use a specific subject when composing a complete email.
- State the purpose, decision, or request in the opening paragraph.
- Put supporting context after the main point.
- Use bullets for multiple questions, requirements, or next steps.
- Make requests explicit, including owner and deadline when relevant.
- Keep greetings and sign-offs appropriate to the relationship.
- End after the necessary context and action; do not restate the message.

Concise does not mean abrupt. Retain enough social context for the audience and
stakes.

## Platform features

Apply the shared rules in [../links.md](../links.md) when the draft references
work artifacts. Email-specific forms:

- In markdown email, use `[label](url)`. In HTML email, use a normal anchor.
- In plain text, prefer `Label <url>` over a bare URL mid-sentence.

Bad: `Can you look at JET-74429?`

Good: `Can you look at [JET-74429](https://.../browse/JET-74429)?`

Bad: `The implementation is in https://github.com/example/lvt_ui.`

Good: `The implementation is in [lvt_ui](https://github.com/example/lvt_ui).`
