# Default

Use this mode when the destination medium is unspecified or unsupported. Do not
ask which medium unless that choice would materially change structure, greeting,
or platform syntax.

Write complete, scannable engineering prose that pastes cleanly into most
workplace tools.

- Lead with the point, decision, request, or result.
- Prefer complete sentences over chat fragments.
- Do not add medium-specific ceremony: no forced greeting or sign-off, no ticket
  template sections, no chat-only slang.
- Keep a greeting or gratitude only when the original used it for real social
  purpose. Delete evaluative acknowledgment or praise.
- Make requests explicit. Keep owner and timing when the original included them.
- Use short paragraphs. Add headings or bullets only when they improve scanning.
- Preserve technical identifiers exactly.
- When link or mention formatting would differ by platform, prefer a plain
  identifier or a readable labeled form already present in the input. Do not
  invent platform syntax.

Most rewrites should stay close to ordinary coworker email or ticket comment
prose: direct, complete, and free of filler.
